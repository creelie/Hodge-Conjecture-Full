# References

The papers below are the Hodge-related part of the author's reference
library. They are listed here with their journal or arXiv identifiers rather
than stored as PDFs, because most are under the publishers' copyright.

The author's own work, which this repository builds on:

- D. Bhattacharjee, *Algebraic Loci of Weil Classes on Abelian Varieties*,
  version 5.1.0, Zenodo (2026), doi:10.5281/zenodo.23227940. Source in `H8/`.

## Weil classes and Prym varieties

- C. Schoen, *Hodge classes on self-products of a variety with an
  automorphism*, Compositio Math. 65 (1988), 3–32.
  numdam.org/item?id=CM_1988__65_1_3_0
- C. Schoen, *Addendum to: Hodge classes on self-products of a variety with
  an automorphism*, Compositio Math. 114 (1998), 329–336.
  Adds a polarization hypothesis to Theorem 3.2 of the 1988 paper and proves
  every Weil fourfold for Q(√−3) through a family of Prym sixfolds.
- K. Koike, *Algebraicity of some Weil Hodge classes*, Canad. Math. Bull. 47
  (2004), 566–572; arXiv:math/0211304. The Prym map of cyclic étale covers of
  degree four of genus-four curves is dominant, giving Weil sixfolds for
  Q(√−1).
- D. Patel and Y. Zhang, *Algebraicity of Hodge classes on some generalized
  Prym varieties*, arXiv:2506.13729. Schoen's cycles from unramified
  geometric class field theory.
- E. Looijenga, *Prym representations of mapping class groups*, Geom.
  Dedicata 64 (1997), 69–83.
- E. Markman, *Secant sheaves on abelian n-folds with real multiplication and
  Weil classes on abelian 2n-folds with complex multiplication*,
  arXiv:2509.23079. Reduces CM fields of higher degree to a semiregularity
  question that the paper leaves open.

## Hodge classes beyond abelian varieties (the statement F3')

- E. Markman, *Rational Hodge isometries of hyper-Kähler varieties of
  K3[n]-type are algebraic*, arXiv:2204.00516.
- F. Charles and E. Markman, *The standard conjectures for holomorphic
  symplectic varieties deformation equivalent to Hilbert schemes of K3
  surfaces*, arXiv:1009.0413.
- J. J. Ramón Marí, *On the Hodge conjecture for products of certain
  surfaces*, Collect. Math. 59 (2008), 1–26, doi:10.1007/BF03191179.
- D. R. Morrison, *On K3 surfaces with large Picard number*, Invent. Math. 75
  (1984), 105–121.
- Z. Xu, *Algebraic cycles on a generalized Kummer variety*,
  arXiv:1506.04297.
- L. Fu, Z. Tian and C. Vial, *Motivic hyperKähler resolution conjecture I:
  generalized Kummer varieties*, arXiv:1608.04968.
- T.-H. Bülles, *Motives of moduli spaces on K3 surfaces and of special
  cubic fourfolds*, arXiv:1806.08284.
- S. Floccari, *On the motive of O'Grady's six-dimensional hyper-Kähler
  varieties*, Épijournal Géom. Algébrique 7 (2023), Article 4;
  arXiv:2203.16257.
- S. Floccari, L. Fu and Z. Zhang, *On the motive of O'Grady's
  ten-dimensional hyper-Kähler varieties*, Commun. Contemp. Math. 23 (2021),
  2050034, doi:10.1142/S0219199720500340.
- S. Floccari, *K3 surfaces associated with varieties of generalized Kummer
  type*, arXiv:2501.02315.
- H. Awada, M. Bolognesi, R. Laterveer and C. Pedrini, *K3 surfaces and
  cubic fourfolds with abelian motive*, arXiv:2012.05969.
- C. Ingalls, A. Logan and O. Patashnick, *Explicit coverings of families of
  elliptic surfaces by squares of curves*, arXiv:2009.07807.

These prove that particular varieties have motives, or Hodge classes, of
abelian type. None of them proves F3' in general.
